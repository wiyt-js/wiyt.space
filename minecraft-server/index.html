Codes of conduct while online

Rules¶

  1. Be respectful towards all players.
  2. No cheating or griefing.
  3. All hate speech, racism, sexism, homophobia and personal hostility will not be tolerated and will result in a ban.
  4. No lag machines, AFK machines are okay.
  5. Harmless pranks and PvP are fine if both parties enjoy them, killing tamed pets will result in a ban.
  6. Staff has final say on any and all issues on the server.
  7. Please have fun!
